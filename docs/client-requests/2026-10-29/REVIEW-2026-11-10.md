## Answer for R27

R27: "When we pick an authorized pickup who is already in the system and has a
PIN, do we have to set a new PIN?" No. Picking the person from the search
links the same person to the child, so their existing PIN works for this
child too. A new PIN is only needed for someone added as a new person.
(To confirm on screen after the deploy.)

## R5 / R8 and step E

Step E (2026-11-10) treats each **classroom** of the Ameris curriculum center
as an age group with its own Lesson Planner. The client now wants the age
groups to be the **Age Range list on the Categories tab** (e.g. "2-3 years").
That means:

- one Ameris Lesson Planner per age range, not per classroom;
- the subscriber's age groups on their account are age ranges;
- each subscriber classroom picks an age range on the Lesson Calendar (R8).

The data change is small: planners and their cells are keyed by classroom
today and would be keyed by age range instead. Existing planner content in
the Ameris classrooms would have to be moved to an age range.
